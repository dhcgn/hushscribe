Confidential speech-to-text

Transcribe confidential audio.

Your recording is encrypted before it leaves this tab and stays encrypted the whole way — including while the AI is transcribing it. Nobody along the chain can read it: not the AI provider, not the cloud it runs on. Not the audio, not the transcript.

Ordinary encryption protects your data in transit and at rest. Confidential computing keeps it unreadable while it is being used. That is the whole reason this page can exist.

New here? Three steps
  1. Get an API key.

    Sign up at portal.privatemode.ai and copy the key it gives you. That key is how transcription gets paid for. It stays in your browser — this page has no server to send it to.

  2. Paste it below and press Verify.

    Your browser then checks, before anything is sent, that it is really talking to a sealed machine and not an impostor. A green check means the check passed.

  3. Drop in your recording.

    Audio or video. It is scrambled here on your device, unscrambled only inside that sealed machine, and the text comes back to you. Pick the spoken language first if you want timestamps.

Nothing you drop here is uploaded to us — there is no “us” to upload to. Transcripts are saved in this browser only, and you can delete them at any time.

Access
Where do I get this?

Sign up at portal.privatemode.ai and copy the key from your account. It works like a password that also pays for transcription, so treat it like one. It is stored in this browser and sent only to Privatemode, never to anyone else.

Transcription
Which should I pick?

Whisper, unless you are in a hurry. It copes better with accents, background noise, and poor recordings. Voxtral is quicker but wants clean audio — if the result looks wrong, run it again with Whisper.

Why does the language matter?

Two reasons. Naming it gets you a better, faster transcript than letting the model guess. And it is the only way to get timestamps — the clickable line-by-line transcript and downloadable subtitles. On automatic detection you still get the full text, just as one block with no times attached.

0 characters

What goes in here?

Anything the model is likely to get wrong: people’s names, company or product names, medical or legal terms, acronyms, unusual spellings. Write them the way you want them to appear, in the same language as the recording. Keep it short — it is a hint, not an instruction, and only the first couple of hundred characters are read.
